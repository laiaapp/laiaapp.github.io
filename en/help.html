Where is my data?

Only on your phone. LAIA has no account and no server; I cannot see your data. More in the privacy policy.

How do I read a lease?

  1. Tap the scan icon at the top right and choose “Import lease” (PDF) or “Scan lease” (camera). While LAIA is still empty, both are offered right on the portfolio.
  2. LAIA recognises address, unit, tenants, rent, deposit and term. You go through the steps and correct whatever does not fit.
  3. At the end LAIA creates the property, unit, tenants and tenancy; the lease is kept as a document.

Recognition runs on the phone. Clear PDF files work best; for photos, even light without shadows helps.

How do I record a rent payment?

In the “Letting” tab under “Rent”, every open item has a tick. Tapping it records the payment; different amounts and part payments go into the “Record payment” sheet.

When is the rent due?

Under Settings → “Renting” you choose whether the rent is due on a day of the month or by a working day. Working days skip the public holidays of the address's country and region – choose the region in the address form.

I have a new phone. Does my data come along?

Not at the moment: the data stays on the old phone. Syncing with a cloud storage of your own comes next.

Which currency does LAIA show?

On first start LAIA takes the currency of your country. You can change it under Settings → “Currency”; stored amounts are not converted.

Does LAIA cost anything?

No. LAIA is free and shows no ads.

Contact

Please send questions, bugs or wishes to laia.app@gmx.de. The app version (Settings → “About LAIA”) and a short description of what you did help a lot.
