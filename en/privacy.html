Privacy policy

Updated: 24.10.2026 · applies to the LAIA app and this website

In short: LAIA stores everything on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The postal code of an address goes – unless you switch it off – to a service that returns the city, and Google's text recognition reports technical usage data to Google, never your documents.

1. Who offers LAIA

LAIA is offered by Viktor Steick as a private individual. Contact: laia.app@gmx.de; the postal address is in the legal notice. The same details are shown on the Google Play listing.

2. Your data stays on the device

Everything you enter in LAIA – properties, units, tenancies, people, payments, dates, documents and photos – is stored only on your phone. I receive none of it; there is no LAIA server.

3. Text recognition and document scanner

LAIA reads leases with Google's text recognition (ML Kit) on the device; the document scanner comes from the phone's Google Play services. Your documents and the recognised text are not uploaded for this.

ML Kit sends technical usage data to Google over an encrypted connection: device information (manufacturer, model, Android version), the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, configuration and size of the input, and error codes. Google uses them for diagnostics and usage analytics of ML Kit and does not share them with third parties (Google's statement: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). I do not receive this data.

4. Place from postal code

When you enter a postal code for an address, LAIA sends the postal code and the country over an encrypted connection to the service zippopotam.us to fill in the city and region. Nothing else is sent. You can switch this off under Settings → “Place from postal code”.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: scanning documents and taking photos of properties and people.
  • Calendar: only if you switch on syncing the planner with a calendar of your own on the device.
  • Notifications: planner reminders, also after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: the postal code lookup.

LAIA asks for each permission only when you use the feature.

6. What LAIA does not do

No account, no sign-in, no advertising. LAIA includes no analytics or crash reporting services; apart from ML Kit's usage data (section 3) nothing goes to third parties. I only see crashes anonymously in Google Play's quality reports, if you have allowed Google to send diagnostic data.

7. Android backup

Android's backup does not include LAIA's data – properties, tenants, payments and documents – and does not move it to a new phone.

8. Deleting data, your rights

You delete entries directly in the app; uninstalling LAIA deletes all of the app's data. As I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to delete or hand over. For questions about your rights under the GDPR, write to laia.app@gmx.de.

9. This website

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When a page is opened, GitHub processes technical data such as the IP address to deliver and protect the pages. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other servers.

10. Changes

If the way LAIA handles data changes, I will update this policy; the date above shows the current version.
