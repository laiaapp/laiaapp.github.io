Test LAIA

Updated: 25.10.2026 · Guide for the closed test

Thank you for helping! Google releases LAIA to everyone only after at least twelve people have been in the test for 14 days in a row. It helps most if you install LAIA, open it now and then and try a few features.

How it works

You need an Android phone and the Google account it uses for the Play Store. It is easiest to open the links on that phone.

  1. Join the tester group: open groups.google.com/g/laiaapp-tester and tap “Join group” – with the Google account of your phone. You will not get any messages from it.
  2. Join the test: open play.google.com/apps/testing/app.laia and tap “Become a tester”. After joining the group it may take a few minutes until this works.
  3. Install: via LAIA on Google Play or by searching for “LAIA” in the Play Store.
  4. Keep it for 14 days: keep LAIA installed and stay in the test for at least 14 days. Open the app now and then – add a property, a tenant or a date, for example, or read in a lease from a PDF. The app is available in English.
  5. Give feedback (optional, but very welcome): on LAIA’s page in the Play Store under “Send feedback to the developer”, or by e-mail to laia.app@gmx.de.

Good to know

  • LAIA is made for phones, not tablets.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. I cannot see your data.
  • Feel free to enter sample data – or your real data if you let property yourself.
  • Updates arrive automatically through the Play Store.
  • You can leave at any time through the test link (“Leave the program”). Please only after the 14 days – otherwise your participation does not count.
